How to read these numbers

The central rate is output currency traded divided by input currency traded during the same GGG hour. These independently observed pair ratios can disagree across a cycle. A positive result is a historical candidate, not proof that the trades were simultaneously available.

Cycle profit uses your starting amount, applies the selected haircut at every trade, and rounds each output down to a whole unit. This is a synthetic unit-fill model; hourly averages do not reveal actual order lot sizes. Intermediate remainders are excluded from the ending amount.

The historical range multiplies the lowest and highest observed pair rates. It is a scenario range before rounding and haircut, not a confidence interval or a guaranteed bound on future returns. Its extrema may have occurred at different times.

The volume filter requires both currencies in every pair to have traded at least the entered amount during that hour. Counts in different currencies are not directly comparable. Historical stock is shown only as context and is never treated as current liquidity. Gold costs are unavailable here and are excluded from profit.

Only pairs with both item IDs under GGG’s Currency category are scanned. Triangular cycles are deduplicated by rotation; the reverse direction remains separate. Unknown currency names display a readable version of their internal identifier.
